# OpenAI Content Sharing Agreement - Client Disclosure

## Overview

Robinson AI Systems uses OpenAI's API services to provide AI-powered features in Cortiware. This document outlines the data sharing implications for our clients.

## Important Notice for Clients

**If you use Cortiware features powered by OpenAI's API, your data may be used by OpenAI for model training and improvement.**

## What This Means

### Data That May Be Shared

When you use AI-powered features in Cortiware (such as AI estimators, query optimization, or other AI-enhanced functionality), the following data may be shared with OpenAI:

- Input text and prompts you provide
- Generated responses and outputs
- Usage patterns and interactions

### OpenAI's Use of Data

According to OpenAI's Content Sharing Agreement with Robinson AI Systems:

> "We may use Customer Content that is designated by your organization owner in your account ('Designated Content') to develop and improve the Services, including for training our models and other research, development, evaluation, and testing purposes ('Development Purposes')."

### What This Means for You

1. **Model Training**: Your data may be used to train and improve OpenAI's models
2. **No DPA Coverage**: Data used for Development Purposes is NOT subject to Data Processing Agreement protections
3. **Independent Controller**: OpenAI processes this data as an independent Data Controller
4. **No Confidentiality**: Do not input sensitive, confidential, or proprietary information into AI features

## Your Responsibilities

As a Cortiware client using OpenAI-powered features, you must:

1. **Obtain Consent**: Ensure you have collected necessary consent from your end users
2. **Provide Notice**: Inform your end users that their data may be used for AI model training
3. **Avoid Sensitive Data**: Do not input:
   - Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA
   - Personal data of children under 13
   - Confidential business information
   - Proprietary data
   - Any information you don't want used for model training

## How to Identify AI-Powered Features

Features powered by OpenAI will be clearly marked with:
- 🤖 AI icon
- "Powered by OpenAI" label
- Wallet/credit requirement (402 guard)

## Opting Out

If you do not want your data used for OpenAI model training:

1. **Do not use AI-powered features** - These features are optional
2. **Contact support** - We can disable AI features for your organization
3. **Use alternative features** - Most Cortiware functionality does not use AI

## Data Protection Measures

For data that IS protected under our standard DPA:

- All non-AI features maintain full DPA protection
- Database storage is encrypted
- Access controls and audit logging are in place
- Standard data processing agreements apply
