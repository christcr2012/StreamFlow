Guidelines
----------
• Use icon-only versions for favicons and app icons. 
• Use full logo (icon + wordmark) for headers, marketing, and presentations.
• Google Workspace Admin uploads work best with JPEG or PNG on a light gray (#F8F9FA) background and no transparency.
• Minimum safe padding = 1x the stroke width around the logo; do not crowd the mark.
